    01 — Profile

    Trained to model systems.
    Employed to make them work.

    I studied industrial engineering at Özyeğin University in Istanbul on a 40% scholarship, with specialisations in finance, manufacturing and service operations management, and transportation and logistics. The degree gave me the modelling half of the job — simulation, optimisation, process design. Everything since has been the other half: running real operations where the data is messy, the sites are live, and someone is waiting on a payroll run.

    Today I sit between operations, product and engineering at a restaurant technology platform, supporting POS, kiosk, digital ordering, menu, payment, payroll, inventory and marketing across 59 live locations for California-based brands, on US time zones. In parallel I produce daily financial and invoice reporting for a co-op reimbursement business serving 350 US automotive dealerships.

    The pattern across every role is the same: learn the system properly, find where the time is actually being lost, and replace the manual step with something that reports itself — a Jira dashboard, a payroll tracker, an n8n approval flow, a forecast built into the client's own reporting.
