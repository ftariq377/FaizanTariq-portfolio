    About

    Trained to model systems.
    Employed to make them work.

    Industrial Engineering at Özyeğin University gave me the modelling half of the job: simulation, optimisation and process design. Every role since has been the other half: running real operations where the data is messy, the sites are live, and someone is waiting on a payroll run.

    Today I'm a Senior Operations Associate at AIO App, a restaurant-technology platform serving 65 live U.S. locations. I lead a four-person team that takes 6–7 new restaurants live every week across menu, payroll, catering, POS, kiosks and DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub. Twelve so far, with zero churn. I own pre-launch QA and lead requirements for a new Catering Module with our product managers.

    Before that: daily financial reporting for 350 U.S. automotive dealerships, an ERPNext rollout, and managing a ten-person team. The through-line is consistent: learn the system properly, find where the time is actually lost, and replace the manual step with something that reports itself.

    Context

    Six or seven newly signed restaurants arrive every week. Each needs menu, payroll, catering where required, marketing, inventory and DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub set up before it can serve a single customer, usually over two to three weeks.

    What I did

    Took ownership of delivery from post-onboarding to go-live: assigning work across a four-person team, personally handling the key accounts, running pre-launch QA across POS, kiosks, online ordering and KitchenHub, and fixing menu issues on accounts that showed churn risk.

    Result

    Twelve restaurants taken live so far, and none of them has churned.

    Context

    Nineteen restaurants, each with its own pay cycle, payday, approval and policy deadlines and time zone. With nothing tracking them in one place, deadlines were being missed: only 50–60% of payroll ran on time.

    What I did

    Worked with the payroll team to build a web-based dashboard, deployed on company servers, that centralises every location's cycle and deadlines and sends automated alerts to the right people before anything is missed.

    Result

    On-time payroll rose to 95%. The remaining misses trace to customer-side, legal or customers' internal issues rather than to tracking.
