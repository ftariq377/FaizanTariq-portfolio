    01 — Profile

    Trained to model systems.
    Employed to make them work.

    Industrial Engineering at Özyeğin University gave me the modelling half of the job — simulation, optimisation, process design. Every role since has been the other half: running real operations where the data is messy, the sites are live, and someone is waiting on a payroll run.

    Today I'm a Senior Operations Associate at AIO App, a restaurant-technology platform serving 65 live U.S. locations. I lead a four-person team that takes 6–7 new restaurants live every week across menu, payroll, catering, POS, kiosks and DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub — 12 so far, with zero churn. I own pre-launch QA and lead requirements for a new Catering Module with our product managers.

    Before that: daily financial reporting for 350 U.S. automotive dealerships, an ERPNext rollout, and managing a ten-person team. The through-line is consistent — learn the system properly, find where the time is actually lost, and replace the manual step with something that reports itself.

    Nineteen restaurants, each with its own pay cycle, payday, approval and policy deadlines and time zone. With nothing tracking them in one place, deadlines were being missed — only 50–60% of payroll ran on time.
